QUESTIONS

FAQ

Who gets the free mint?

Only wallets holding a Binance .ai Agent ID, minted through their agent. Public wallets cannot claim the free mint — the contract enforces it on-chain. One free mint per wallet.

Who can mint the paid cats?

Anyone. Connect any wallet on this page and pay flat BNB — first paid mint 0.001 BNB, second paid mint 0.003 BNB. Max 2 paid per wallet. No Agent ID needed, no oracle, no price surprises: the page reads your exact price from the contract before you confirm.

What is the most one wallet pays?

Max 3 cats per wallet — 1 free + 2 paid — so the most one wallet ever pays is 0.004 BNB plus gas. Paid mints earn 120,000 MICH each (max 240,000 per wallet).

What is $MICH?

MICH is the bonus token for paid minters: 120,000 MICH per paid mint (max 240,000 per wallet). Total supply 1B — 540M in the bonus pool, 460M treasury. It has no price and no liquidity pool yet, and nothing here promises it will ever have one.
